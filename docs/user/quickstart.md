# Quickstart Guide

Welcome to Rapua. Follow these five steps to set up and launch your first game.
Whether you are hosting a team-building event, an educational exercise, or a
community activity, Rapua makes the process quick and straightforward.

This guide expands on the Quickstart Bar that appears when you first sign up or
when you create a new quest.

![](/static/images/docs/quickstart-bar.webp)

---

## Step 1: Create a Quest

Every game begins with a quest. A quest is the container for your game, where
you build the objectives, set the rules, and manage teams.

1. [Log in](/login) to your Rapua account.
2. Go to your [Quests](/admin/quests) and click **Create New Quest**.
3. Give it a name, for example "Orientation" or "Health and Safety Tour".
4. Save the quest to proceed.

You are now ready to add objectives, content, teams, and settings.

**Tip:** Your current quest is shown on the dashboard. The fastest way to switch
between quests is to click the quest name and pick another from the dropdown.

---

## Step 2: Add Objectives

Objectives are the things players do. An objective can be a place to visit, a
question to answer, a task to complete, or anything else you can describe with
content blocks.

1. Go to the [Quest](/admin/quest) tab.
2. Click **New objective** and give it a title, for example "The Library".
3. Open it and add content blocks: text and images to set the scene, and at
   least one interactive block (a quiz, a password, a scan) if players have to
   prove they were there.
4. Save, and repeat for everything you want players to do.

New objectives start as drafts, which means players cannot see them yet. Publish
one with the eye icon when it is ready.

**Tip:** Add at least three objectives to start with. That is enough to see how
routing and grouping behave in the next step.

Drag an objective onto another to nest it. An objective with things inside it
becomes a [section](/docs/user/sections), which is how you build chapters,
themed areas, or "any one of these will do" choices.

---

## Step 3: Add Teams

Organise participants into teams for collaboration or competition.

1. Go to the [Teams](/admin/runs) tab.
2. Click **Add Team** and specify how many teams you want.
3. Save, and repeat as needed.

Invite participants by sharing the team codes. Teams can be added at any time,
so there is no need to settle this now. Players start by entering their team
code on the [home page](/).

---

## Step 4: Update Game Settings

Customise how the game plays.

1. Open the [Quest](/admin/quest) tab.
2. Set the routing on the quest itself, which decides how players move through
   the objectives at the top level:
   - **Guided Path:** one at a time, in order.
   - **Open Exploration:** all of them, in any order.
   - **Randomised Route:** a few at a time, chosen per team.
3. Turn **Points** on or off with the toggle beside the routing picker.
4. Look at the [Game Settings](/docs/user/game-settings) page for the rest,
   including the leaderboard and what players see when they finish.

Each section you create sets its own routing the same way, so a guided quest can
still contain an area players explore freely.

**Tip:** Hover over the information icons for guidance. Each setting changes how
the game feels, so it is worth reading them.

---

## Step 5: Start (or Schedule) the Game

Your game is ready to launch.

1. Go to the [Activity](/admin/activity) page.
2. Choose one of the following:
   - **Start:** launch the game immediately.
   - **Schedule:** pick a date and time for an automatic launch.
3. Share the team codes with participants.

That is it. Your game is live, and participants are ready to explore and learn.

Once a game is running the editor is locked, because a change reaches players
straight away and cannot be taken back. Stop the game to make changes, or
duplicate the quest and work on the copy.
